## The entry

> If anyone asks, I died doing what I loved: buffering a video you weren't even watching.

Why it should win: it is the phone itself talking, in its own voice, about its own dying battery — and the punch lands on the last beat. The phone does not beg for a charger or read out a will; it dies a hero's death doing the one job nobody asked it to do, buffering a video its owner was not even watching. The twist reframes the whole line: the tragedy is not the battery, it is the pointlessness of the sacrifice.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with no trailing newline.
